Flip write direction and tile image. Use when the aircraft's DataRef is wired inverted (e.g. AW109 fuel xfeed: +1 visually up, −1 visually down — opposite to most switches). Vertical: Min ↦ switch up, Max ↦ switch down. Pair short / long press with UP/DOWN buttons.
Horizontal: Min ↦ switch left, Max ↦ switch right. Pair short / long press with LEFT/RIGHT buttons.
Invert Direction swaps both at once: short press steps toward Min instead of Max, long press toward Max instead of Min, and the tile shows Min on the bottom / Max on the top (or right / left when horizontal). Static Label is baked into the switch image (e.g. "BAT") and stays put regardless of position. Labels override the Stream Deck title with the current position (e.g. "OFF" / "ON"). Use them together for a button labeled "BAT" with a dynamic "OFF" status. Suppress the green OK tick on every successful press. Don't show the red alert when pressing past Min / Max — just ignore.
