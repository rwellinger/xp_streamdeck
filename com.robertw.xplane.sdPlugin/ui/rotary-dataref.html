LEFT and DOWN subtract delta; RIGHT and UP add delta. Pair two or four buttons on the Stream Deck to drive one setpoint.
Leave Min/Max empty for unbounded setpoints. With both set, the value clamps at the endstops (unless Cycle is checked) and the button flashes the alert icon when already at the limit (unless suppressed below).
Requires Min and Max. Example: Min 0, Max 30, Delta 10 → 0→10→20→30→0.
Long-press (coarse step)
If set, a long press (≥500 ms) uses this larger step instead of delta. Useful to drive a setpoint coarse with the same button (e.g. HDG bug ±1 short / ±10 long).
